Continuous take with a home stand

Task: put the mug in the sink. Cameras keep recording; boundaries are detected. Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, click the timeline to seek.

Episode boundaries

  • Home: both cubes within ~3 cm / 15° of their docked pose and still ≥ 0.5 s → idle.
  • Knock the grippers together → audio sync point + episode start.
  • Back on the stand and still → episode ends.
  • Reset by hand with grippers docked → stays idle, not recorded as an episode.
  • Failure: show card 24 to the head camera → episode discarded.

Home stand

  • Base ~35 × 15 cm, fixed to the counter for the session.
  • Two cradles: grippers drop in at the same pose every time (left on the left).
  • Tags 30 / 31 / 36 (8 cm) on a strip tilted ~45° toward the head, behind the cradles so docked grippers don't hide them.
  • Tags are part of the environment body: they also add a second, tilted plane to the world frame.
