  • Tags flat, not bent, glued rigidly
  • Each gripper has its own finger-tag pair (left/right grippers differ)

2Time sync (start of every session)

  1. On a phone or monitor, open the GoPro Labs Precision Date and Time QR page (gopro.github.io/labs). It shows a QR code that updates continuously.
  2. Turn on GoPro A and point it at the QR code until it confirms (beep or screen).
  3. Repeat for every other GoPro, including any extra non-gripper camera.
  4. Re-sync after a battery swap or a long power-off. recommended

Accuracy is about ±1/60 s. The pipeline groups videos into demos and aligns frames using this timecode. Without sync, bimanual episodes are mismatched or dropped.

To verify: film the QR code, then run python scripts/calibrate_timecode_offset.py -i video.MP4

3Mapping video (1 per session)

Before

  • Arrange the scene as it will be for the demos
  • Place tag 13 flat on the table, near the center of the workspace
  • Use a gripper camera (mounted on the gripper), not a bare GoPro

Record

  1. Start recording 0.3–2 m from the tag, with the tag near the image center. Hold a few seconds. recommended
  2. Move slowly over the whole workspace, covering every height and angle the demos will use.
  3. Include close views at grasp height and wide views of the surroundings.
  4. Return to the tag several times along the way. Several good tag views make the world frame more accurate.
  5. Stop after about 60 s. recommended

Rules

Tag 13 seen 0.3–2 m away, in the central zonerequired
Slow, smooth motion; no fast spinsrequired
Scene must have texture; avoid looking at blank walls onlyrequired
Don't move objects or furniture until the session endsrecommended

After mapping, the tag can stay or be removed. Tags are inpainted out of the training images.

4Gripper calibration video (1 per gripper camera)

  1. Start recording on the gripper camera.
  2. Close the gripper fully and hold about 1 s.
  3. Open it fully and hold about 1 s.
  4. Repeat 3–5 times, then stop. recommended
Both finger tags detected in ≥ 80 % of framesrequired
Reach the true fully closed and fully open positionsrequired
Record before the first demo of the sessionrecommended

The minimum width measured becomes 0 and the maximum becomes fully open (calibrate_gripper_range.py). If the gripper never fully closes in this video, every width in the dataset is offset.

5Demonstrations (N per session)

Each demo

  1. Reset the scene to a new starting state.
  2. Hold the gripper so the camera sees the mapped workspace.
  3. Start recording. Wait about 1 s with the camera on the scene. recommended
  4. Do the task once, at normal speed and with smooth motion.
  5. Hold about 1 s at the end, then stop recording.

Rules

One video = one demorequired
Stay in the area covered by the mapping videorequired
≤ 10 untracked frames per video, else the whole video is droppedrequired
≥ 60 tracked frames (≈1 s)required
Finger tags visible in ≥ 90 % of framesrecommended
No hand or object covering the camera for longrecommended
Vary object positions and starting poses across demosrecommended
How many demos: plan for 10–20 % more than you need. Videos with lost tracking get dropped. The pipeline prints the share of data it kept (the example dataset kept 99 %).

Copy the original MP4s and don't re-encode them. The IMU data and timecode live inside the MP4 metadata.

Naming mapping.mp4Picks the largest file as the mapping video

Place both explicitly. Relying on the fallback is how a long demo becomes the "map" or a demo becomes the "gripper calibration".

Session checklist

  • All cameras: 2.7K 4:3, 60 fps, Lens = Wide, HyperSmooth off, orientation locked
  • QR time sync done on every camera
  • Mapping video with tag 13 in clear view, about 60 s, slow motion
  • Gripper calibration video per gripper camera, fully closed and fully open
  • N demos, one video each, scene reset between demos
  • Files placed as raw_videos/mapping.mp4 and raw_videos/gripper_calibration/
  • run_slam_pipeline.py → check data used % → 07 … --no_mirror
